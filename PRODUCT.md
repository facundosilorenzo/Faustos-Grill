# Product

## What this is
Website for **Fausto's Grill**, a grill restaurant (carnes a la brasa) at Calle Victoria 86, Málaga, Spain. Open Tuesday to Sunday 9:00–23:30 with a non-stop kitchen (breakfast through dinner); closed Mondays. Phone +34 952 00 62 61.

## Audience
- Locals from the Victoria / Lagunillas / centro neighbourhoods deciding where to eat today, mostly on a phone.
- Tourists in Málaga searching "grill / carne a la brasa near me", arriving from Google Maps.
- Groups planning a dinner or celebration.

## Goals (in order)
1. Get a table: call or send a reservation request in seconds.
2. Show the food: menu with prices and real photos.
3. Build trust: Google rating and reviews.
4. Make it easy to arrive: address, map, directions, hours and whether it's open right now.

## Voice
Spanish from Spain, *tú*. Warm, direct, unpretentious. Short sentences. No marketing superlatives; let the product speak ("al punto que pidas", "la brasa no se apaga hasta el cierre").

## Surface mode
Persuade (impeccable): the opening must make the offer clear and expose the working action (reserve / call / open now).

## Constraints
- Static site, no build step, no dependencies. Content must stay visible if JavaScript fails.
- Never present example reviews as real ones.
